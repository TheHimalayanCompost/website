Benefits & Nutrients

The same enriched formula throughout — but how you apply it changes by use. Pick a category below for exact steps and dosage.

Plants

Enhances growth, improves soil structure, promotes vibrant blooms.

  1. Loosen the top layer of soil with a hand fork or trowel.
  2. Add 1–2 handfuls (roughly 100–200g) for a 6–8" pot.
  3. Mix it gently into the topsoil.
  4. Water lightly so nutrients settle in.
  5. Repeat every 3–4 weeks.

Trees

Strengthens roots, boosts drought resistance, improves nutrient uptake.

  1. Loosen the soil in a ring around the base, at the drip line.
  2. Apply 1–2kg for young trees, up to 4–5kg for large, mature trees.
  3. Keep it a few inches clear of the trunk itself.
  4. Water thoroughly so it reaches the root zone.
  5. Repeat every 2–3 months during the growing season.

Crop / Vegetable

Increases yield, enhances soil fertility, reduces chemical fertilizer use.

  1. Loosen the soil around the base of each plant, or across the bed.
  2. Apply 200–300g per plant, or 2–3kg per 10 sq. meters for beds.
  3. Mix lightly into the top few centimeters of soil.
  4. Water immediately after application.
  5. Repeat every 3–4 weeks through the growing season.

Lawns

Promotes lush green coverage, improves soil aeration, supports turf health.

  1. Mow the lawn short and lightly rake or aerate the surface.
  2. Spread 2–3kg of vermicompost evenly per 10 sq. meters.
  3. Water lightly to help it settle into the soil.
  4. Repeat every 2–3 months during active growing seasons.
